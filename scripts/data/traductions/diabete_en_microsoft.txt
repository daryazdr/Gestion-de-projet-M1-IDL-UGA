**Foods to avoid**

You should avoid the following foods:

**Recommended Foods**

You should eat the following foods:
- Animal protein: at least 1 serving per day
  - Meats: beef, veal, lamb, pork, chicken, rabbit, guinea fowl, turkey, turkey
  - Fish: all except swordfish, shrimp, raw shellfish, raw fish, smoked fish
  - Eggs
- Dairy: at least 4 per day
  - Cheeses except raw milk cheeses, industrial grated cheeses, cheese rinds
  - Milk: semi-skimmed
  - Dairy: plain, unsweetened
- Vegetables and fruits: at every meal
  - Green vegetables: fresh, frozen natural
  - Fruit: all allowed, 1 fruit for dessert with each meal
- Starchy foods: at every meal
  - Bread: or rusks, sandwich bread
  - Cereals: pasta, rice, semolina, polenta, pilpil, couscous
  - Potato: mashed, steamed, hash browns, fries
  - Pulses: peas, flageolets, lentils, chickpeas, corn, broad beans, white or red beans
