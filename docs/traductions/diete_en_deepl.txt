FOODS TO AVOID
Sugar, honey, jams, jellies, chestnut cream, Nutella.
  
Sweets, toffees, nougat, confectionery.
 -   Chocolate, fruit jellies. 
Pastries, biscuits, Viennese pastries (croissants, chocolate bread, brioche, etc.).
Dried fruit, candied fruit. 
Yoghurt, fromage blanc and fruit-flavoured Petit Suisse, custards, desserts.
Ice cream, sorbets. 
Sweetened condensed milk.
Fruit juices, syrups, fizzy drinks, Coca-Cola, all commercially available sweetened drinks. 
All alcoholic drinks.
Soya and soya-based products (tofu)
Acidic or spicy foods.

RECOMMENDED FOODS
ANIMAL PROTEINS: at least 1 portion per day			
		
         Meat: beef, veal, lamb, pork, chicken, rabbit, guinea fowl, turkey, young turkey.
If you are not immune to toxoplasmosis, eat meat that is thoroughly cooked; avoid rillettes, pâtés, foie gras and meat in jelly.
	    Fish: all except swordfish, prawns, raw shellfish, raw fish (sushi, tarama, surimi), smoked fish (herring, salmon, trout). Choose oily fish: tuna, salmon, mackerel, sardines.
         Eggs 
 
DAIRY PRODUCTS: At least 4 per day  plus in dishes
 
Cheeses except: raw milk cheeses, industrial grated cheeses, cheese rinds.
 Milk: semi-skimmed.
 Dairy products: plain, unsweetened.
	           
FRUIT AND VEGETABLES:  With every meal
.
 Green vegetables: fresh or frozen, plain: as much as you like.
 Fruit:  All allowed, 1 piece of fruit for dessert with every meal, 
One portion of fruit is equivalent to  4 sugar cubes   
= ½ melon,    1 large slice of watermelon (250 g) 
= 1 small punnet (250g) of strawberries, raspberries, redcurrants, blackberries,  blueberries, blackcurrants, … 
= 2 mandarins, 4 ‘Corsican’ clementines, 2 kiwis, 1 small grapefruit
= 1 pear, 1 apple, 1 orange, 1 peach or nectarine
= 3 to 4 apricots, damsons, Reine Claude plums, etc., 8 mirabelle plums, 10 fresh lychees
= ¼ pineapple or 4 slices, ½ mango or 1 small one
= 12 to 15 grapes or cherries, 1 small or ½ banana
= 2 to 3 dates, 3 to 4 prunes, 2 to 3 fresh figs 
= 1 small bowl of compote
STARCHY FOODS: At every meal
Bread: or  rusks, sandwich bread …
Cereals: pasta, rice, semolina, polenta, pilpil, couscous…  
Potatoes: mashed, steamed, fried, chips…
Pulses: peas, flageolet beans, lentils, chickpeas, sweetcorn, broad beans, white or red beans, split peas and cassava, green plantains, yams…
