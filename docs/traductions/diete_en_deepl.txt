Prohibited foods (do not eat) 
For your health, you should not eat sugar. 
Do not eat these sugary products: 
• Sugar, honey and jam. 
• Nutella and chestnut cream. 
• Sweets, chocolates and pastries. 
• Biscuits and pastries (croissants, brioches).
• Dried fruit and candied fruit. 
Do not drink these beverages: 
• Fruit juices and syrups. 
• Soft drinks (such as Coca-Cola). 
• Alcoholic beverages. 
Also avoid: 
• Soya. 
• Very spicy or acidic foods (chilli, lemon).

Recommended foods 
Meat, fish and eggs 
Eat protein at least once a day.
Meat:
• You can eat: beef, veal, pork, chicken, rabbit, turkey. 
• Warning: If you are not protected against toxoplasmosis, meat must be cooked thoroughly. 
• Do not eat: rillettes, pâté, foie gras or meat in jelly.
Fish: 
• You can eat almost all types of fish. 
• Oily fish are very good for your health (tuna, salmon, sardines, mackerel). 
• Do not eat: swordfish, prawns, raw fish (sushi), surimi or smoked fish.
 Eggs: 
•You can eat eggs. 

Milk and dairy products 
Eat 4 dairy products per day. 
• You can eat: semi-skimmed milk, plain yoghurt or plain fromage blanc.
• Cheese: You can eat cheese. 
• Do not eat: raw milk cheese, grated cheese in packets or cheese rinds.

Vegetables and fruit 
You should eat these with every meal. 
Vegetables: 
• Eat as many green vegetables as you like (fresh or frozen). 
Fruit:
• Eat 1 piece of fruit for dessert with every meal. 
• This is what "1 portion of fruit" means: 
	o 1 apple, 1 pear, 1 orange or 1 peach. 
	o 2 kiwis or 2 mandarins. 
	o 1 small punnet of strawberries or raspberries. 
	o 12 grapes or 12 cherries. o Half a banana. 
	o A small bowl of unsweetened compote. 

Starch / Carbs foods 
Starchy foods give you energy. Eat them with every meal.
• Bread: wholemeal bread, sandwich bread or rusks. 
• Cereals: pasta, rice, semolina, polenta, couscous. 
• Potatoes: steamed, mashed or baked. 
• Pulses: lentils, chickpeas, white or red beans, peas, sweetcorn.

My daily meals (Example) 
Morning (breakfast): 
• 1 drink: tea, coffee or cocoa (without sugar). 
• 1 dairy product (milk or plain yoghurt). 
• Wholemeal bread or sugar-free cereal with a little butter. 
Lunch and dinner: 
• Green vegetables (as much as you like).
• One portion of meat, fish, eggs or ham. 
• One energy portion of your choice: 
	o One plate of starchy foods (pasta, rice, etc.) AND a small piece of bread. 
	o OR one large plate of starchy foods (without bread).
	o OR a large piece of bread (without starchy foods). 
• 1 dairy product (cheese or plain yoghurt).
• 1 piece of fruit. 
If you feel hungry between meals (snack): Do not eat sugar. 
You can eat: 
• Plain yoghurt. 
• Raw vegetables (carrots, cucumber).
• A hard-boiled egg or a slice of ham.
