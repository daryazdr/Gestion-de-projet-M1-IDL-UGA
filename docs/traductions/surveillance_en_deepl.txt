Before you begin: 
Wash your hands with warm water and soap. 
Dry your hands thoroughly. 
Caution: do not use alcohol or cream on your hands.

How to prick your finger: 
When taking a blood sample, you should massage the finger you are going to prick. 

Where to prick: 
Do not prick your thumb or index finger. 
Do not prick the tip of your finger. 
Prick the side of your finger. 
You should use a different finger and a different spot each time.

Caution: use a new needle each time you take a blood sample. The needle should only be used once. 

When should you do the test? 
You should do 6 tests per day: 
In the morning: as soon as you wake up, before eating or drinking. 
After breakfast: 2 hours after the start of the meal. 
Before lunch.
After lunch: 2 hours after the start of the meal.
Before dinner. 
After dinner: 2 hours after the start of the meal. 

Results 
It is important to record the results. 
Before meals, the figure should be less than 0.95 g/L. 
Two hours after meals, the figure should be less than 1.20 g/L.
