AVOID
Sugar, honey, jams, jellies, chestnut cream, Nutella.
  
Sweets, caramels, nougat, confectionery.
 - Chocolate, fruit jellies. 
Pastries, biscuits, pastries (croissant, chcocolat bread, brioche, etc.).
Dried fruits, candied fruits. 
Yoghurt, cottage cheese and fruit biscuits, flans, desserts.
Ice creams, sorbets. 
Sweetened condensed milk.
Fruit juices, syrups, sodas, Coca-Cola, all sugary drinks from the store. 
All alcoholic beverages.
Soya and soy products (tofu)
Acidic or spicy foods.
                                 FOOD

RECOMMENDED
ANIMAL PROTEIN: at least 1 serving per day			
		
Meats: beef, veal, lamb, pork, chicken, rabbit, guinea fowl, turkey, poult.
If you are not immune to toxoplasmosis, eat well-cooked meats, avoid rillettes, pâtés, foie gras, jellied meats.
	    Fish: all except swordfish, shrimps, raw shellfish, raw fish (sushi, taramasalata, surimi), smoked fish (herring, salmon, trout). Favor blue fish: tuna, salmon, mackerel, sardines.
         Eggs 
	   	
DARY: At least 4 per day plus in preparation
		
Cheeses except: raw milk cheeses, industrial grated cheeses, cheese rinds.
         Milk: semi-skimmed.
         Dairy: plain, unsweetened.
	           
VEGETABLES AND FRUITS: At every meal
.
         Green vegetables: fresh, frozen au naturel: as much as you like.
         Fruit: All allowed, 1 fruit for dessert with each meal,

= 1 small punnet (250g) of strawberries, raspberries, redcurrants, blackberries, blueberries, blackcurrants, ...                                                                                               
= 2 mandarins, 4 "Corsican" clementines, 2 kiwis, 1 small grapefruit
= 1 pear, 1 apple, 1 orange, 1 peach or brugnon
= 3 to 4 apricots, plums, queens Claude ..., 8 mirabelle plums, 10 fresh lychees
= 1/4 pineapple or 4 rings, 1/2 mango or 1 small
= 12 to 15 grapes or cherries, 1 small or 1/2 banana
= 2 to 3 dates, 3 to 4 prunes, 2 to 3 fresh figs                                                 
= 1 Compote cup
STARCHY FOODS: At every meal
Bread: or rusks, sandwich bread....
Cereals: pasta, rice, semolina, polenta, pilpil, couscous...  
Potato: mashed potatoes, steamed, hash browns, fries...
Pulses: peas, flageolet beans, lentils, chickpeas, maize, broad beans, white or red beans, split peas and cassava, green bananas, yams, etc.
