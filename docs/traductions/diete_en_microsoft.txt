Prohibited
For your health, you should not eat sugar.
Don't eat these sugary products:
• Sugar, honey and jam.
• Nutella and chestnut cream.
• Sweets, chocolates and pastries.
• Biscuits and pastries (croissants, brioches).
• Dried fruits and candied fruits.
Don't drink these drinks:
• Fruit juices and syrups.
• Sodas (such as Coca-Cola).
• Drinks with alcohol.
Also avoid:
• Soy.
• Very spicy or acidic foods (chili, lemon).

Food

recommended
Meat, fish and eggs
Eat protein at least once a day.
Meat:
• You can eat: beef, veal, pork, chicken, rabbit, turkey.
• Caution: If you are not protected against toxoplasmosis, the meat should be very cooked.
• Do not eat: rillettes, pâté, foie gras or jellied meat.
Fish:
• You can eat almost any fish.
• "Blue" fish are very good for your health (tuna, salmon, sardines, mackerel).
• Don't eat: swordfish, shrimp, raw fish (sushi), surimi or smoked fish.
Eggs:
• You can eat eggs.

Milk and dairy products
Eat 4 dairy products a day.
• You can eat: semi-skimmed milk, plain yoghurt or plain cottage cheese.
• Cheese: You can eat cheese.
• Do not eat: raw milk cheese, grated cheese in a bag or the rind of the cheese.

Vegetables and fruits
You have to eat it at every meal.
Vegetables:
• Eat as many greens as you want (fresh or frozen).
Fruits:
• Eat 1 piece of fruit for dessert at each meal.
• Here is what "1 serving of fruit" means:
o 1 apple, 1 pear, 1 orange or 1 peach.
o 2 kiwis or 2 mandarins.
o 1 small punnet of strawberries or raspberries.
o 12 grapes or 12 cherries.
o Half a banana.
o A small cup of sugar-free compote.

Starchy foods
Starchy foods give you energy. Eat it at every meal.
• Bread: wholemeal bread, sandwich bread or rusks.
• Cereals: pasta, rice, semolina, polenta, couscous.
• Potatoes: steamed, mashed or baked.
• Pulses: lentils, chickpeas, white or red beans, peas, corn.

Typical day
In the morning (Breakfast):
• 1 drink: tea, coffee or cocoa (without sugar).
• 1 dairy product (milk or plain yoghurt).
• Wholemeal bread or sugar-free cereal with a little butter.
Lunch and dinner (Lunch and Dinner):
• Greens (as many as you want).
• A portion of meat, fish, egg or ham.
• A portion of energy, of your choice:
o A plate of starchy foods (pasta, rice, etc.) AND a small piece of bread.
o OR a large plate of starchy foods (without bread).
o OR a large piece of bread (without starches).
• 1 dairy product (cheese or plain yoghurt).
• 1 fruit.
If you are hungry between meals (Snack): Do not eat sugar. You can eat:
• A plain yoghurt.
• Raw vegetables (carrots, cucumbers).
• A hard-boiled egg or a slice of ham.
